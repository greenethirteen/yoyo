Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

YOYO is a study app for students. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use YOYO.

Information we collect

YOYO may process information you provide while using the app, such as study activity, answers and progress data, to provide the app's features. We do not sell personal information.

AI features

If an AI-powered lesson feature is used, the question and relevant study information may be sent to our server to generate an explanation. The service provider may process that information to provide the requested response.

Third-party services

YOYO may use third-party services for hosting, analytics or AI-powered features. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children and students

YOYO is intended for students. We do not knowingly sell personal information or use the app to build advertising profiles about students.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the app and comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask questions about your information or request deletion where applicable.
